Installing an **LED screen in a gym** has become an essential tool for amateur sports clubs. From showcasing partners to enhancing match-day atmosphere and professionalizing the club's image, the benefits are numerous. Here's everything you need to know.

## Why install a screen in your gym?

A screen in a gym isn't just for displaying the score. It transforms the spectator experience and creates value for your sponsors:

- **Partner visibility**: broadcast advertising spots before, during and after matches
- **Match animation**: player announcements, game highlights, celebrations
- **Club communication**: upcoming events, results, merchandise
- **Professional image**: spectators enjoy an experience worthy of professional clubs

## What screen size should you choose?

For a standard-sized gym (handball, basketball, volleyball), here are the recommendations:

| Gym capacity | Recommended size | Minimum resolution |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Under 300 seats | 55 to 65 inches | Full HD (1080p) |
| 300 to 800 seats | 75 to 85 inches | Full HD (1080p) |
| Over 800 seats | 2 screens or LED wall | 4K recommended |

The key is that the screen must be **visible from all stands**. Positioning it high up, slightly angled toward the audience, is ideal.

## How much does it cost?

The budget depends on the screen you choose:

- **65" TV screen**: 500 to 1,000 EUR
- **75" professional screen**: 1,000 to 2,000 EUR
- **85" professional screen**: 2,000 to 3,500 EUR

On top of this comes the **digital signage solution** to manage content. At Neopro, subscriptions start at **1,500 EUR/year excl. VAT** and include all the software, the remote control app and video templates.

## How to finance the installation?

The good news: **the screen can be funded by your partners**. Here are the three most common approaches:

1. **Existing sponsors**: offer them digital visibility alongside traditional banners. Most will accept a moderate surcharge to appear on screen.
2. **New partners**: the screen attracts businesses that weren't interested in static banners or boards.
3. **Municipal grants**: some local authorities fund equipment for municipal sports halls. Check with your local sports department.
