The term **"digital signage"** may sound like something reserved for professional clubs. However, solutions have become more accessible and are now available to amateur clubs — from lower divisions to regional championships. Here are the 7 concrete benefits you can gain.

## 1. Professionalize the match experience

It's a guaranteed "wow" effect. When spectators arrive at your gym and see a **screen broadcasting professional animations**, the perception changes instantly.

Players are also impacted: seeing their celebrations or introductions on screen creates a sense of pride and belonging. Just like in professional clubs.

## 2. Create a new advertising space

Are your courtside banners all sold? With a digital screen, you create an **additional advertising space** without any physical modifications:

- Spot rotation during matches
- Personalized messages per partner
- Broadcasting also possible during training sessions and tournaments

It's simply a new revenue stream.

## 3. Simplify match-day management

Without digital signage, match day is often a rush: managing music, announcements on the microphone, keeping score... With a signage solution, you **program everything in advance**:

- Pre-match loop: partner spots + program of the day
- Timeouts: automatic animations
- Halftime: dedicated content
- Post-match: thank-yous and upcoming events

On the day, a single volunteer with their phone is enough to run everything.

## 4. Work without wifi

This is often the number one barrier: *"We don't have wifi in the gym."* Good digital signage solutions work **without an internet connection**. The box creates its own local network, and the phone connects to it directly.

No wifi needed. No network cable. Just a screen and a power outlet.

## 5. Attract new partners

Local businesses are increasingly reluctant to sponsor static banners. On the other hand, an **animated spot on a digital screen** immediately interests them:

- It's modern and visible
- It's measurable (number of plays, broadcast duration)
- It's adaptable (content updated for each match)

Several clubs report attracting partners **who would never have signed up** for a traditional banner.

## 6. Offer attractive video packages

Beyond simple logo display, digital signage lets you offer **professional video content**:

- Player announcements with visual effects
- Personalized celebrations
- Animated partner spots featuring their latest news

This content can also be shared on social media, which multiplies the value for the partner.

## 7. Quantify and prove visibility

Reporting changes the relationship with your sponsors. Instead of saying *"your logo was displayed"*, you can say:

> *"Your spot was broadcast 47 times this season, for a total duration of 23 minutes, in front of an estimated cumulative audience of 4,200 spectators."*

This level of professionalism is what turns a one-time sponsor into a **loyal partner over multiple seasons**.
