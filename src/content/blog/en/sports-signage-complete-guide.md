## What is sports signage?

**Sports signage** is the set of technical and human tools that allow a club to manage content broadcast during a match. This includes:

- Displaying the **score** and **game clock**
- Broadcasting **sponsor spots** on screen(s)
- **Visual animations** (team entrances, celebrations, timeouts, halftime)
- **Background music** and sound effects
- **Announcements** to spectators (lineups, game actions, thank-yous)

In a professional club, these functions are often managed by a dedicated team with professional equipment. In an amateur or semi-pro club, it's often improvised: one volunteer with a phone for music, another for the score, a microphone for announcements... with all the mishaps that come with it on match day.

## What is sports signage used for in a club?

### 1. Professionalize the match experience

It's a guaranteed "wow" effect. When spectators arrive at your gym and see a **screen broadcasting animations**, the real-time score and animated sponsor spots, the perception changes instantly. Your players see their photos on screen as they take the court. Just like in a pro club.

### 2. Create a new advertising space

Are your courtside banners all sold? With digital sports signage, you create **a new advertising inventory** without any physical modifications. Each match becomes a scheduled broadcast window for your partners, with rotation, game-time targeting and reporting included.

### 3. Retain and attract sponsors

Local businesses are increasingly reluctant to sponsor static banners. On the other hand, an **animated spot on a digital screen** is modern, visible, measurable and shareable on social media. Many clubs report attracting sponsors who would never have signed up for a traditional banner.

### 4. Simplify match-day organization

On the day, the digital sports signage is controlled by **a single volunteer with their smartphone**. Everything is pre-programmed: the pre-match loop, sponsor spots, celebration animations, halftime sequences, the final thank-you. No need for a team of 5 people to coordinate.

### 5. Prove ROI to partners

Instead of saying *"your logo was displayed"*, you say: *"your spot was broadcast 47 times this season, for a total duration of 23 minutes, in front of an estimated cumulative audience of 4,200 spectators."* This level of reporting is what turns a one-time sponsor into a **loyal partner over multiple seasons**.
