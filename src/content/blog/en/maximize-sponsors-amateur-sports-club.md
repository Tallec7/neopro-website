Finding sponsors is hard. **Keeping them is even harder.** Yet most amateur clubs settle for sticking a logo on a banner and forgetting about it for a year. The result: disappointed partners who don't renew.

Here are 5 practical methods to deliver real value to your sponsors — and retain them.

## 1. Move from static banners to digital screens

A courtside banner is fine. An **animated spot on a digital screen** is better. The difference in perception is immediate:

- Motion catches the eye (unlike a banner nobody looks at)
- You can rotate multiple sponsors on the same screen
- Content can be updated every week (news, promotions, partner events)
- The screen also works outside match days (training sessions, tournaments, events)

With a digital signage solution, each sponsor benefits from **visible and measurable rotations** during matches.

## 2. Offer visibility on social media

Your sponsors want to reach people, not just the 200 spectators in the gym. Integrate them into your digital communications:

- **Instagram Stories**: mention your sponsors in pre-match stories
- **Dedicated posts**: one thank-you post per month for each sponsor
- **Matchday videos**: include sponsor logos in your match highlights

It's free and it significantly multiplies the reach of their investment.

## 3. Offer targeted naming opportunities

Beyond classic sponsorship, propose **specific activations**:

- The **"sponsored timeout"**: the sponsor's spot plays during every timeout
- The **"player of the match presented by..."**: the sponsor appears on the MVP announcement
- The **"halftime by..."**: the sponsor is associated with the halftime entertainment

These targeted formats justify a higher price and give the partner premium visibility.

## 4. Produce quality videos

Visual quality changes everything. A sponsor who sees their logo in a **professional video template** has a completely different perception than a logo stuck on an A4 sheet.

Video packages typically include:

- Animated partner loops
- Player announcements with the club's visual identity
- Personalized messages for premium sponsors
- Branded celebrations and game highlights

## 5. Provide visibility reporting

The key factor: **proving ROI** to your partners. With a digital signage system, you can provide:

- The number of plays for each spot
- Total broadcast time per match
- Number of matches covered in the season
- Photo/video proof

A partner who receives a visibility report at the end of the season is a partner who **renews**.
